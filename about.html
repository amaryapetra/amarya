About

Hi, I'm Amarya.

I'm pursuing a B.S. in Mathematics and Economics alongside a B.A. in Jewish Studies at Tulane University, graduating in 2027. My coursework spans proof-based mathematics, econometrics, and Jewish history and thought, and I'm especially interested in the places where rigorous quantitative analysis and humanistic inquiry intersect.

I'm a Tulane Honors Scholar, and much of my time outside class goes to teaching and mentoring. I'm a Supplemental Instructor for economics, and I lead the Afterschool Newcomb Tutoring Club, which provides free weekly tutoring to students across New Orleans. I also mentor first-year honors students through Newcomb-Tulane College.

I grew up in New York and spent a gap year in Israel before coming to Tulane. In my spare time, I enjoy cooking and meal planning.
