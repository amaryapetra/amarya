Background and Interests

I am pursuing a B.S. in Mathematics and Economics and a B.A. in Jewish Studies at Tulane University, with an expected graduation date of May 2027. My coursework spans proof-based mathematics, econometrics, and Jewish history and culture, and I am particularly interested in the intersection of rigorous quantitative analysis and humanistic inquiry.

As a Tulane Honors Scholar, I dedicate much of my time outside the classroom to teaching and mentoring. I serve as a Supplemental Instructor for economics and as President of the Afterschool Newcomb Tutoring Club, which provides free weekly tutoring to students throughout New Orleans. I also mentor first-year honors students through Newcomb-Tulane College.

Originally from New York, I completed a gap year program in Israel before enrolling at Tulane. Outside of my academic work, I enjoy cooking.
