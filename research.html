Honors Thesis & Research

Friendship as Social Capital: Jewish Identity and Economic Opportunity

Provisional title · Expected 2027

Advisors: Dr. Toni Weiss and Dr. Ilana Horwitz

Economics Jewish Studies

My honors thesis looks at friendship as a form of social capital. I'm comparing the college friendship networks of Jewish and non-Jewish students — who is in them, and what information, resources, and professional opportunities those relationships open up — to understand how identity shapes access to economic opportunity.

The project sits right where my two fields meet: it brings economic research on social networks and mobility into conversation with Jewish Studies, using network analysis to look at how these patterns form over the college years.

I'm always glad to talk more about this project.
